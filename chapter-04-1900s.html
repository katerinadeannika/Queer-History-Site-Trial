The Scientist, the Star, and the Suit

1900–1909. Hirschfeld’s reform network, Colette and Missy’s theater, sexology’s double edge, and gender performance in Berlin and Paris.

Recognition and regulation

Sexological language could offer recognition and arguments against criminalization, while also making people objects of expert classification. Hirschfeld’s Scientific-Humanitarian Committee campaigned against Paragraph 175; the Institute for Sexual Science opened only in 1919.

Performance

Colette and Mathilde de Morny, known as Missy, made costume, celebrity, domestic intimacy, and artistic collaboration publicly visible. Their 1907 Moulin Rouge performance of Rêve d’Égypte became controversial under police pressure.
