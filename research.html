Research, access, and production practice

Archive, Rights & Research Toolkit

This page turns the series’ research notes into a practical guide for reading, sourcing, fact-checking, and eventual audiovisual clearance.

Research access is not production permission. A digitized image, online film, catalog record, or public web page may be available for viewing while still requiring permission for public reproduction, adaptation, distribution, or commercial use.

Core principles

Confirm the item

Rights differ among items in the same repository. Record the exact title, creator, date, collection, item ID, and URL.

Separate the layers

A public-domain film may have a copyrighted restoration, score, subtitle track, colorization, scan, or upload.

Read archives critically

Police, court, medical, propaganda, and exposé records document systems of power as well as the people they describe.

Consult custodians

Work with community archives and living custodians, especially for marginalized, colonized, displaced, Black, and transgender histories.

Relationship evidence standard

Evidence levelEditorial useDo not infer from
Documented partnershipState directly when reciprocal correspondence, shared household/business evidence, legal documentation, or strong scholarly consensus supports it.A single photo or a shared address alone.
Strongly supported intimacyAttribute and qualify substantial but incomplete or contested evidence.Costume, role, or fan interpretation.
Documented attachment or friendshipDescribe affection, mentorship, friendship, or one-sided desire without making a sexual claim.A queer reading of fiction or performance.
Rumor or later speculationOmit unless the history of the rumor itself is necessary; never state it as fact.Studio gossip, publicity photography, or later assertion alone.

AssetTitle, date, creator, original item ID, and repository.

License scopeMedia, duration, territory, platforms, language versions, marketing, social media, and derivative uses.
