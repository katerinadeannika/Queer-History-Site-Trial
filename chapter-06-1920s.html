The City Speaks Back

1920–1929. Berlin, Paris, Harlem, Tokyo, Shanghai, and Los Angeles created uneven worlds of magazines, salons, performance, homes, bars, and activism. Visibility was not equality: law, racism, class, policing, and citizenship shaped who could be seen safely.
