Before the Name

1880–1889. Victorian law, public order, urban anonymity, early sexology, and the city worlds of London, Paris, Berlin, and Capri.

Law and city life

The law did not create desire; it created new risks. Britain’s 1885 Labouchere Amendment broadened prosecution for gross indecency between men. Paragraph 175 remained a tool against men in the German Empire. Paris had no general sodomy statute, but policing and public-order rules still shaped behavior and visibility.

People and media

Oscar Wilde’s rising public career and John Addington Symonds’s writing belong to a wider world of theaters, railways, lodging houses, print, travel, and unequal class protections. Coded art and public style are subjects for interpretation, not automatic biography.
