Why 1880?

This series begins in the 1880s not because queer people began then, but because modern city life, criminal law, mass print, sexology, empire, and visual media produced a dense—though deeply uneven—archive.

Reading the archive

Official records frequently document institutions policing, classifying, or criminalizing people. Read them alongside letters, literature, performance, material culture, oral history, and community records. A photograph, arrest, or court record may document a public event; it does not automatically establish identity, consent, desire, or a private relationship.
