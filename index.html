Contents

This website presents the working manuscript Before the Name: A Queer History in Ten Decades, 1880–1969 under its public-facing title, Early Queer History. It preserves the project’s source links, evidence standards, production notes, and rights cautions.

Archive note. A police record may document an arrest, not a person’s self-understanding. A photograph may document a public image, not private sexual conduct. Links are research and discovery leads; they are not automatic reproduction permissions.
