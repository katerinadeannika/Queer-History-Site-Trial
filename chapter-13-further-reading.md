## Further Reading
<a name="further-reading"></a>

This guide consolidates core scholarly reading for the TV series. Use the bibliography for full Chicago-style citations and archival/institutional sources. Several biographies, memoirs, and popular histories are valuable entry points but should be corroborated for specific claims about private relationships, legal history, and contested events.

### General, legal, and urban history

- Chauncey, George. *Gay New York: Gender, Urban Culture, and the Making of the Gay Male World, 1890–1940.*
- Cocks, H. G. *Nameless Offences: Homosexual Desire in the Nineteenth Century.*
- Cook, Matt. *London and the Culture of Homosexuality, 1885–1914.*
- D’Emilio, John. *Sexual Politics, Sexual Communities: The Making of a Homosexual Minority in the United States, 1940–1970.*
- Faderman, Lillian. *Odd Girls and Twilight Lovers: A History of Lesbian Life in Twentieth-Century America.*
- Weeks, Jeffrey. *Sex, Politics and Society: The Regulation of Sexuality Since 1800.*
